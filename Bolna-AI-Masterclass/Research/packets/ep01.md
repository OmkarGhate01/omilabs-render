# Packet: Ep 1 What Is Bolna AI? The Complete Beginner Overview

## Voice agents (agents/overview)

Learn how to build, deploy, and monitor Bolna Voice AI agents that hold natural phone and web conversations in 40+ languages.

A Bolna voice agent listens to a caller, decides what to say, and answers in a natural voice — on inbound calls, outbound calls, batch campaigns, or in the browser. Every agent follows the same lifecycle: **build** it, **deploy** it to a phone number or app, then **monitor** what happens on each call. When it works in testing, **go live** with the launch checklist.

**Build**:
    Set the prompt, LLM, voice, languages, tools and knowledge base in Agent Studio, or design a multi-step flow with graph agents.

**Deploy**:
    Buy or connect phone numbers, place outbound, batch and workflow calls, receive inbound calls, or bring your own SIP trunk.

**Monitor**:
    Review call history, receive webhooks, pull executions over the API, and extract structured data from every call.

**Go live**:
    Test before calling real customers, control cost per call, handle failures at scale, and troubleshoot common problems.

## Featured

**Graph agents**:
    Part of **Build**. Design a multi-step conversation as typed nodes and routing rules instead of one long prompt.

**Workflows (beta)**:
    Part of **Deploy**. Chain calls, WhatsApp messages, conditions and retries into one sequence per contact, and run it over contact lists as campaigns.

Not sure which one you need? See [graph agents vs workflows](/docs/graph-agent/graph-agents-vs-workflows).

## How a call works

A standard agent chains three services: a **transcriber** turns the caller's audio into text, an **LLM** decides what to say, and a **synthesizer** turns the reply back into audio. A [speech-to-speech](/docs/speech-to-speech) agent replaces all three with a single realtime model. See [how a call flows through Bolna](/docs/concepts/call-flow) for the full lifecycle, from dialing to the finished execution record.

## How to decide

**Start in the dashboard if you are evaluating**:
    Agent Studio gets you to a real call fastest, and everything it produces is visible and editable in the [agent builder](/docs/agent-setup/overview).

**Start with the API if calls are triggered by your systems**:
    Anything you can do in the dashboard is available over the [API](/docs/api-reference/introduction), including creating agents, placing calls, and pulling transcripts.

**Choose a graph agent when the flow is strict**:
    Use it when the conversation must follow defined states — verification, eligibility checks, scripted collections. See [Graph agents vs workflows](/docs/graph-agent/graph-agents-vs-workflows).

**Choose a no-code tool when the trigger lives elsewhere**:
    If the call should fire from a spreadsheet or CRM event, use [Make.com, n8n, Zapier, viaSocket or Google Sheets](/docs/tutorials/introduction) instead of writing a backend.

  These are not exclusive. A common pattern is building and tuning the agent in the dashboard, then triggering calls in production over the [Make call API](/docs/api-reference/calls/make) or in bulk with [batches](/docs/outbound/batch-calling).

## What every path still needs

Whichever route you take, you will need a phone number and a telephony provider before real calls work. Bolna can sell you a number, or you can bring your own Twilio, Plivo, Exotel or Vobiz account, or your own SIP trunk — see [Choose a telephony setup](/docs/supported-telephony-providers).
