# Packet: Ep 5 Bolna vs Traditional IVR and Moving From Other Voice Platforms

## Moving to Bolna from another voice AI platform (get-started/coming-from-another-platform)

Map the concepts you already know — assistants, phone numbers, tools, call logs, webhooks — onto Bolna's agents, executions, and extractions, and port an existing agent.

If you have already built a voice agent elsewhere, most of what you know transfers. The vocabulary changes and a few things sit in different places.

## Differences worth knowing before you port anything

**Wait for `completed`, not the disconnect event**:
    `call-disconnected` fires the moment the line drops, before duration, cost, recording and extracted data are populated. The `completed` status arrives a few seconds later with everything filled in. See [Call statuses](/docs/post-call/list-phone-call-status).

**Keys are per workspace, not per agent**:
    Provider credentials are added once under [Connect provider credentials](/docs/providers) and reused across agents.

**Telephony is a choice, not a default**:
    You can buy a number from Bolna, connect Twilio, Plivo, Exotel or Vobiz, or register your own SIP trunk. India-specific paths (140/160-series numbers, DLT) are documented separately.

**Indian languages are first-class**:
    Per-language transcription and voice providers are configurable on one agent — see [Set up a multilingual agent](/docs/customizations/multilingual-languages-support).

## Porting an agent

**Recreate the agent**:
    Paste your existing system prompt into the Agent tab, or send it to [`POST /v2/agent`](/docs/api-reference/agent/v2/create). Start with the [preferred models](/docs/pricing/preferred-models) rather than matching your old provider exactly — swap later if quality demands it.

**Re-declare your tools**:
    Tools are declared per agent on the [Tools tab](/docs/agent-setup/tools-tab). Call transfer and calendar actions are built in; anything else is a [custom API call](/docs/tool-calling/custom-function-calls).

**Point your number at Bolna**:
    Either [buy a number](/docs/buying-phone-numbers) or connect the account or trunk your number already lives on.

**Re-point your webhook**:
    Set the webhook URL on the [Extractions tab](/docs/agent-setup/analytics-tab) and whitelist Bolna's three source IPs — see [Get results with webhooks](/docs/post-call/polling-call-status-webhooks).

**Compare on real calls**:
    Run the same 20 calls through both systems before switching traffic. [Test before you call real customers](/docs/production/test-before-calling-customers) covers what to check.

  Migrating a large agent estate? Write it once against the [API](/docs/api-reference/introduction) or drive it from the [CLI](/docs/cli/introduction) rather than rebuilding by hand in the dashboard.
