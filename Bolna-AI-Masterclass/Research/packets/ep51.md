# Packet: Ep 51 Production Readiness Checklist and Deployment

## Deploy your voice agent (agents/deploy)

Put a Bolna Voice AI agent in front of callers: get phone numbers, place outbound and batch calls, answer inbound calls, connect telephony, or embed web calls.

Once your agent is configured, choose how people reach it. Most teams start with a Bolna phone number and outbound calls, then add inbound calling, batch campaigns, or their own telephony as they scale.

## Get a phone number

**Buy a number**:
    Purchase a US or Indian number from the dashboard and attach it to an agent.

**140 & 160-series**:
    Get regulated series numbers for promotional and transactional calling in India.

**Truecaller verification**:
    Show your business name and logo to the people you call.

Before buying numbers on Bolna, submit a one-time [compliance application](/docs/compliance-application/introduction). See the documents required and [how to submit them](/docs/compliance-application/how-to-submit-guide).

## Make and receive calls

**Outbound calls**:
    Place a single call from the dashboard or `POST /call`.

**Batch calls**:
    Upload a CSV and schedule a calling campaign.

**Workflows (beta)**:
    Run multi-step sequences such as call, retry, then follow up on WhatsApp, over a contact list as a campaign.

**Inbound calls**:
    Link a number to an agent so it answers every incoming call.

**Web calls**:
    Let users talk to your agent from the browser over WebRTC. See the [starter templates](/docs/developer-resources/sdks/web-call-templates) and [SDK reference](/docs/developer-resources/sdks/web-call-api).

New to calling? Follow the [inbound quickstart](/docs/quickstarts/inbound) or the [batch calling API quickstart](/docs/quickstarts/batch). Inbound agents can also [route callers through an IVR](/docs/inbound/ivr), [identify callers](/docs/customizations/identify-incoming-callers) and [block unwanted numbers](/docs/customizations/block-incoming-callers).

Keep campaigns healthy with [auto-retry](/docs/outbound/auto-retry) for calls that did not connect and [calling guardrails](/docs/outbound/calling-guardrails) to restrict calls to allowed hours. During a call you can [hang up programmatically](/docs/outbound/hangup-calls) or collect [keypad input](/docs/inbound/dtmf).

## Connect your own telephony

Use your existing carrier account instead of a Bolna number. See [telephony setup](/docs/supported-telephony-providers) to compare the options.

**Twilio**: Connect your Twilio account.

**Plivo**: Connect your Plivo account.

**Exotel**: Connect your Exotel account.

**Vobiz**: Connect your Vobiz account.

**SIP trunk**: Bring any SIP trunk (BYOT).

For a SIP trunk, start with [setup](/docs/sip-trunking/byot-setup), then use the [API examples and field reference](/docs/sip-trunking/api-examples) to create it.

## Before you go live

Work through the [launch checklist](/docs/production/readiness-checklist) and [test your agent](/docs/production/test-before-calling-customers) before it calls real customers.

## Production readiness checklist for voice AI agents (production/readiness-checklist)

Everything to verify before a Bolna Voice AI agent starts calling real customers: telephony, compliance, quality, observability, concurrency, cost, and failure handling.

Run this before the first campaign, not after the first incident. Each item links to the page that does the work.

## Telephony and numbers

* [ ] Production number is provisioned — [bought from Bolna](/docs/buying-phone-numbers), or on your own [Twilio](/docs/twilio), [Plivo](/docs/plivo), [Exotel](/docs/exotel), [Vobiz](/docs/vobiz) account or [SIP trunk](/docs/sip-trunking/byot-setup)
* [ ] Inbound calls are routed to the right agent — [Route inbound callers](/docs/agent-setup/inbound-tab)
* [ ] Caller ID displays correctly at the destination, including internationally
* [ ] For India: DLT and number-series requirements met — [India DLT compliance](/docs/compliance-application/introduction); [Truecaller verification](/docs/truecaller-verification) if branding matters

## Conversation quality

* [ ] At least 20 test calls made on the production configuration — [Test before you call real customers](/docs/production/test-before-calling-customers)
* [ ] Interruption and latency settings tuned on real calls — [Tune interruptions and speed](/docs/agent-setup/engine-tab)
* [ ] Transcriber verified against your actual vocabulary and languages — [The agent mishears words](/docs/troubleshooting/agent-mishears-words)
* [ ] Escalation path tested end to end — [Transfer a call to a human](/docs/tool-calling/transfer-calls)
* [ ] Agent behaves sensibly on voicemail, wrong numbers, and silence — [End a call automatically](/docs/outbound/hangup-calls)

## Compliance and guardrails

* [ ] Calling-hour restrictions enabled — [Set guardrails and quiet hours](/docs/outbound/calling-guardrails)
* [ ] Do-not-call handling in place — [Block unwanted callers](/docs/customizations/block-incoming-callers)
* [ ] Recording, retention and data-residency requirements confirmed — [Security and data handling](/docs/concepts/security), [Where your data lives](/docs/enterprise/data-residency)

## Observability

* [ ] Webhook endpoint live, with all three Bolna IPs whitelisted — [Get results with webhooks](/docs/post-call/polling-call-status-webhooks)
* [ ] Your handler acts on `completed`, not `call-disconnected`
* [ ] Extractions defined for the fields the business actually needs — [Extract data from conversations](/docs/prompting/using-extractions)
* [ ] Someone owns a daily review of failed calls — [Calls fail or drop early](/docs/troubleshooting/calls-fail-or-drop-early)

## Scale and cost

* [ ] Concurrency requirement calculated and provisioned — [Plan concurrency](/docs/enterprise/concurrency-management)
* [ ] Cost per call measured on real traffic, not estimated — [Control cost per call](/docs/production/control-cost-per-call)
* [ ] Wallet balance monitored, with an alert before it can block calls
* [ ] Retry policy defined — [Handle failures and retries at scale](/docs/production/failures-and-retries-at-scale)

## Access

* [ ] Team members invited with the right roles — [Manage your organization and team](/docs/enterprise/organization)
* [ ] Client or business-unit separation set up if needed — [Manage multiple clients](/docs/enterprise/sub-accounts)
* [ ] API keys rotated out of anything shared during development

  Ship the first campaign small and deliberately: a few hundred calls to a known segment, reviewed manually the same day. The checklist catches configuration gaps; only real traffic catches prompt gaps.
