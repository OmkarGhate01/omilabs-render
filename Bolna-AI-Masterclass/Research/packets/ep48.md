## Ask Bolna: the AI assistant in your dashboard (ask-bolna)

Ask Bolna answers questions about your own agents, calls and batches, explains Bolna features from the docs, and helps you write prompts, right inside the dashboard.

Ask Bolna is an AI assistant built into the Bolna dashboard. It can look up data in your account, search the Bolna documentation for you, and help with voice AI questions such as writing a prompt. It is in beta and available to every Bolna customer.

**Open Ask Bolna**:
  Go to platform.bolna.ai/ask-bolna and sign in with your Bolna account.

## What you can ask

| Kind of question | What Ask Bolna does | Example |
| - | - | - |
| Your own account | Looks up your agents, calls, batches, phone numbers, SIP trunks, knowledge bases, wallet and usage through the [Bolna MCP server](/docs/mcp-server). It never guesses account data. | "How many calls did my agents handle this week?" |
| How Bolna works | Searches the Bolna documentation and links to the pages it used. | "How do I connect my Twilio number?" |
| General voice AI | Answers directly, for example improving an agent prompt, picking a voice or language, or designing a call flow. | "How can I make my agent's greeting sound friendlier?" |

Ask Bolna only sees the account you are signed in to, and it replies in the language you write in.

## Actions that change your account

Some requests change your account, cost money or place real calls, such as buying a number, starting a call or batch, or creating, updating or deleting something. Before any of these, Ask Bolna tells you exactly what it will do and waits for you to confirm in your next message. Read-only lookups do not need confirmation.

## Getting around the dashboard

Ask Bolna can open the right dashboard page for you, such as an agent's call history or the SIP trunks page, or point to the matching item in the sidebar. After each answer it also suggests a few follow-up questions you can click.

## Chat history and feedback

* **Chat history:** your conversations are saved. Reopen a past conversation to see it again or continue where you left off.
* **Feedback:** share feedback on any conversation to tell the Bolna team what worked and what didn't.

## Daily usage limit

Each user can use up to **200,000 tokens a day** with Ask Bolna, counting both your messages and its replies. The limit resets at **12:00 AM IST** (midnight India time). If you reach it, Ask Bolna tells you, and you can continue the next day.

## Building agents

**Why is my agent restricted or marked as disallowed content?**:
    All agents created on Bolna undergo internal compliance and safety checks.

    If you see a message such as **“Agent is restricted due to disallowed content. Please review and update it.”**, it means your agent’s configuration or prompt may have triggered a violation of Bolna’s content safety policies.

    Disallowed content includes (but is not limited to):

    * Political campaigns
    * Illegal activities or solicitation
    * Scam or fraud-related behavior
    * Profanity or hate speech
    * NSFW, adult, or sexually explicit content
    * Harmful or misleading information

    To resolve this, please review and modify your agent’s prompt, instructions, or behavior to ensure it adheres to a valid use-case.
    Once updated, you can re-save the agent to re-trigger validation.

**What type of voice AI agents can I create on Bolna?**:
    Bolna supports a wide range of customizable voice agents. From free-flowing conversational assistants to structured IVR-style bots.

    You can build agents for use cases like [lead qualification](/docs/agent-templates), customer support, interviews, [appointment bookings](/docs/tool-calling/book-calendar-slots), [call transfers](/docs/tool-calling/transfer-calls), and more.

    Get started with our [Agent template library](/docs/agent-templates) or explore the [Agent Studio setup guide](/docs/agent-setup/agent-tab).

**Does Bolna support multilingual Voice agents?**:
    Yes. Bolna supports multiple languages and voices. You can create agents in various languages (e.g., English, Hindi) using built-in multilingual support across [speech-to-text](/docs/deepgram-transcriber), [LLM](/docs/openai-llm), and [text-to-speech](/docs/elevenlabs-voice) components.

    Find the [list of all supported languages](/docs/customizations/multilingual-languages-support) and learn how to [write prompts for multilingual agents](/docs/writing-prompts-in-non-english-languages).

**Can I build my own application using Bolna APIs?**:
    Yes, definitely. Bolna AI is an API-first platform providing a comprehensive API suite to:

    * Create, update, list, and delete voice agents via [Agent APIs](/docs/api-reference/agent/v2/overview).
    * Trigger calls via [Call APIs](/docs/api-reference/calls/overview).
    * Manage executions and logs via [Executions APIs](/docs/api-reference/executions/overview).
    * Do bulk calls using batches via [Batches APIs](/docs/api-reference/batches/overview).
    * Manage phone numbers via [Phone numbers APIs](/docs/api-reference/phone-numbers/overview).
    * Create, list and manage sub‑accounts via [Sub-Account APIs](/docs/api-reference/sub-accounts/overview).

## Pricing and concurrency

**What is the pricing for Bolna Voice AI?**:
    Bolna offers transparent usage-based pricing:

    * **Call pricing**: \$0.02/min platform fee (plus provider charges).

    Please refer to the [cost & pricing documentation](/docs/pricing/call-pricing) for detailed information. For high-volume usage, explore our [Enterprise Plan](/docs/enterprise/plan) with customized volume-based discounts.

**Why is my bill higher than the flat per-minute rate?**:
    Your flat rate only covers a specific set of **preferred ASR, LLM, and TTS models**. If your agent uses a model outside that list, that component is billed separately at variable, usage-based rates instead of being included in the flat rate.

    Check the **Add Funds** panel (click the **+** next to your wallet balance in [Agent Studio](https://platform.bolna.ai/dashboard)) for the current preferred model list, or see the [Preferred models documentation](/docs/pricing/preferred-models) for the full breakdown.

**How many parallel calls can I make in Bolna?**:
    By default, Bolna allows up to **10 concurrent calls** for paid users. Learn more about [outbound calling concurrency](/docs/pricing/outbound-calling-concurrency) or request higher limits via the [Enterprise Plan](/docs/enterprise/plan) for large-scale deployments and [batch calling](/docs/outbound/batch-calling) capabilities.

## Phone numbers and telephony

**Does Bolna support SIP connectivity?**:
    Yes. You can [bring your own SIP trunk](/docs/sip-trunking/introduction) to Bolna and use your existing SIP provider and phone numbers for inbound and outbound calls. See [Set up and register your trunk](/docs/sip-trunking/byot-setup) to get started.

    You can also use [Twilio](/docs/twilio/connect-provider), [Plivo](/docs/plivo/connect-provider), [Exotel](/docs/exotel/connect-provider) or [Vobiz](/docs/vobiz/connect-provider) for telephony.

## What to include when reporting an issue

The faster we can reproduce a problem, the faster we can fix it. Include the following details depending on the issue type.

**Agent behavior issues**:
    * Agent ID and agent type (single-prompt or [graph agent](/docs/graph-agent/introduction))
    * What you expected the agent to do vs. what it actually did
    * Steps to reproduce, including any inputs or user\_data variables passed
    * The relevant `execution_id` and conversation transcript, if the issue happened on a live or test call

**Call quality issues**:
    * The `execution_id` (call ID), phone number, and timestamp of the call
    * Direction (inbound/outbound) and telephony provider used
    * A description of the issue — latency, dropped audio, wrong transcription, premature hangup, etc.
    * Raw logs for the call, pulled via [Get Execution Raw Logs](/docs/api-reference/executions/get_execution_raw_logs) or the **Raw logs** tab under **Monitor → Debugging** in the dashboard

**Platform / dashboard issues**:
    * Browser and version
    * Steps to reproduce
    * Screenshots or the exact error message shown
    * The account email associated with your Bolna workspace

**Batch calling issues**:
    * The `batch_id` and affected recipient row(s) from your CSV
    * Expected vs. actual outcome for those recipients
    * Whether the issue affects the whole batch or specific recipients — see [Batch Calling](/docs/outbound/batch-calling)

## Audio is choppy, robotic, or only one side can hear (troubleshooting/choppy-or-one-way-audio)

Diagnose choppy, robotic or one-way audio on Bolna Voice AI calls across buffer size, telephony provider and SIP trunk configuration.

Audio faults are almost always carrier or transport problems, not agent configuration. Establish which of the three symptoms you have before changing settings.

## First, classify the symptom

| Symptom | Most likely cause |
| - | - |
| Agent audio stutters or drops syllables | Buffer size too low, or packet loss on the carrier leg |
| Agent sounds robotic or metallic throughout | Voice model or codec, not the network |
| Caller hears the agent but the agent hears nothing (or vice versa) | One-way media — SIP or NAT configuration |
| Audio is fine on some calls and bad on others, in one region | Carrier routing for that region |

## Choppy or stuttering agent audio

**Raise buffer size**:
    On the [Languages tab](/docs/agent-setup/audio-tab), increase **Buffer Size**. Values between **150 and 250** work for real-time conversation; lower values start playback sooner but break up under any jitter.

**Check whether it correlates with a provider**:
    Compare recent calls in [Call history](/docs/agent-setup/call-history). If choppiness clusters on one telephony provider or one destination country, it is a carrier issue — see [Choose a telephony setup](/docs/supported-telephony-providers).

**Try a lower-latency voice model**:
    Heavier TTS models are more sensitive to timing. A turbo or low-latency model from the same provider usually removes the stutter.

  Raising buffer size trades delay for smoothness. If callers then complain the agent is slow to reply, you have moved the problem — see [Responses feel slow](/docs/concepts/latency).

## One-way audio

One-way audio on a **bring-your-own-trunk** setup is nearly always media negotiation. Work through:

**Verify the trunk is registered and reachable**:
    Re-check the trunk against [Set up and register your trunk](/docs/sip-trunking/byot-setup).

**Confirm media is allowed in both directions**:
    Firewalls that permit SIP signalling but not RTP produce exactly this symptom. Open the media ports your SBC uses, in both directions.

**Check codec overlap**:
    If the two sides share no codec, the call connects and stays silent.

**Test the same agent on a Bolna-managed number**:
    If audio is clean there, the agent is fine and the trunk is the variable. See [Place calls on your trunk](/docs/sip-trunking/byot-outbound-calls) and [Receive calls on your trunk](/docs/sip-trunking/byot-inbound-calls).

## Background noise, not audio faults

If callers complain the agent's line "sounds noisy", check whether **ambient noise** is enabled on the [Call tab](/docs/agent-setup/call-tab) — it plays a background track deliberately, and is available on Plivo and Vobiz telephony. Set it to **None** to remove it.

## What to collect before contacting support

* The `execution_id` of two bad calls and one good one
* The telephony provider and destination country
* Whether the fault is inbound, outbound, or both
* For SIP trunks, a SIP trace covering the failing call

Then reach out through [Get help](/docs/getting-help).

## Troubleshoot Bolna voice AI calls (troubleshooting/overview)

Find the fix for common Bolna Voice AI call problems: interruptions, mishearing, choppy audio, failed or dropped calls, failed transfers and missing webhooks.

Pick the symptom that matches what you see. Each guide lists the likely causes and the settings to check.

**The agent interrupts people**:
    The agent cuts callers off mid-sentence, or does not stop talking when interrupted.

**The agent mishears words**:
    Names, numbers or Indian language words are transcribed wrongly.

**Audio is choppy or one-way**:
    Playback is choppy or robotic, or only one side can hear.

**Calls fail or drop early**:
    Calls never connect or end sooner than expected.

**Call transfer did not work**:
    A transfer never fires, fails to connect or drops the caller.

**My webhook never arrived**:
    Your server does not receive call events from Bolna.

To see why a specific call ended, check its [call status](/docs/post-call/list-phone-call-status) and [hangup code](/docs/post-call/list-phone-call-hangup-status).
