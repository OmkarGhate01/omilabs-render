# Packet: Ep 4 What a Bolna Call Really Costs: Pricing, Wallet and Plans

## What a Bolna voice AI call costs (get-started/what-it-costs)

A one-screen summary of Bolna Voice AI pricing: the flat per-minute rate, what sits outside it, telephony charges, and where agent and concurrency costs apply.

A short answer for evaluators. The detailed breakdowns live in [Call pricing](/docs/pricing/call-pricing), [Agent pricing](/docs/pricing/agent-pricing) and [Preferred models](/docs/pricing/preferred-models).

## The short version

**Voice AI**:
    Transcription + LLM + text-to-speech. Covered by a **flat per-minute rate** as long as your agent uses [preferred models](/docs/pricing/preferred-models).

**Telephony**:
    Charged per minute by the carrier, and it varies by country and provider. Billed by Bolna, or by your own provider if you [bring your own account](/docs/supported-telephony-providers).

**Platform fee**:
    Bolna's own per-minute component. See [Call pricing](/docs/pricing/call-pricing) for how the three parts add up.

  Billing is usage-based and prepaid from your wallet. Larger top-ups move you to a lower effective per-minute rate.

## What pushes the cost up

| Choice | Effect |
| - | - |
| A model outside the preferred list | That component leaves the flat rate and is billed at variable provider rates — the most common reason two similar agents cost differently. See [Preferred models](/docs/pricing/preferred-models). |
| Long calls | Every component except the LLM is billed by duration. Tightening the prompt and setting a `call_terminate` limit in the [Call tab](/docs/agent-setup/call-tab) is the fastest lever. |
| Verbose LLM output | The LLM is billed by tokens generated. |
| Premium voices and international numbers | Both are priced above the standard bundle. |

For a working method rather than a table, see [Control cost per call](/docs/production/control-cost-per-call).

## What is not billed per minute

* **Agents themselves** — see [Agent pricing](/docs/pricing/agent-pricing) for how many agents a workspace can hold.
* **Concurrency** — how many calls you can run simultaneously is an account setting, not a per-call charge. See [Plan concurrency](/docs/enterprise/concurrency-management).

## Bolna voice AI usage pricing (pricing/call-pricing)

Discover detailed insights into Bolna Voice AI's pricing structure. Learn about cost breakdowns and flexible plans tailored to your business needs.

## How much does Bolna voice AI cost?

For a one-screen summary, see [What it costs](/docs/get-started/what-it-costs). This page breaks down each component of a call's cost.

**View pricing plans**:
  See all available plans, volume tiers, and per-minute rates on our pricing page.

## What does call pricing depend on?

Every call's total cost is the sum of five components across three parts.

**Part A - Voice AI (STT + LLM + TTS)**

**Transcriber**:
    Your choice of Speech-to-Text (STT) model and provider.

    Billed by **call duration** (rounded to seconds).

**LLM**:
    Your choice of Large Language Model (LLM) and provider.

    Billed by **tokens generated**.

**Text to speech (TTS)**:
    Your choice of TTS model and provider.

    Billed by **characters synthesized**.

**Part B & C - Telephony and Platform**

**Telephony provider**:
    Your telephony provider and the country/region of the phone numbers.

    Billed by **call duration** (rounded to minutes).

**Bolna platform fee**:
    A flat per-minute fee charged by Bolna on top of your provider costs.

    Billed by **call duration**.

## Which models are included in my rate?

Every workspace's flat per-minute rate already bundles a curated set of ASR, LLM, and TTS models — Bolna calls these your **preferred models**. Using only preferred models keeps your Voice AI cost at the flat rate; picking any other model bills that component separately at variable, usage-based rates.

**See your preferred models**:
  View the current preferred ASR, LLM, and TTS model list and how it affects billing.

## How can I reduce my voice AI costs?

You can significantly reduce costs by connecting your own provider accounts. When you bring your own keys (BYOK), Bolna does not charge for those components. You only pay your providers directly, plus Bolna's platform fee.

**Connect your provider accounts**:
    Head to [Settings → Integrations](https://platform.bolna.ai/providers) to link your own telephony, LLM, TTS, STT and tool accounts.

**Choose cost-efficient models**:
    Lighter LLM models (e.g. `gpt-4o-mini`) and efficient TTS voices can meaningfully lower per-call costs.

**Choose a volume plan**:
    For predictable workloads, a volume-based plan offers better per-minute rates than pay-as-you-go.
    See available tiers at [bolna.ai/pricing](https://www.bolna.ai/pricing).

[Learn more about supported providers](/docs/providers) and how to connect your accounts.

### BYOK on the basic plan

On the Basic plan, the flat per-minute rate bundles Bolna-paid STT, LLM, and TTS. If your own key serves **any one** of these components on a call, that call is billed on base token pricing instead of the flat per-minute rate. This applies whether you bring one, two, or all three.

* **Your BYOK components:** billed directly by your provider, not by Bolna.
* **Remaining Bolna-provided components:** billed at base usage rates (transcription by duration, LLM by tokens, TTS by characters).
* **Telephony and platform fee:** charged as usual.

Other plans keep their negotiated rate regardless of BYOK, except when all three of STT, LLM, and TTS run on your own keys. In that case the bundled rate doesn't apply and you pay only the platform fee, plus telephony if you use Bolna-hosted numbers.

## Where can I see my call costs?

After each conversation, go to the [Call History page](https://platform.bolna.ai/agent-executions) to see what the conversation cost. The summary cards show **Total Cost** and **Avg Cost** for the selected agent and date range, and each execution row has its own **Cost** column.

For payment receipts, see [how to download your invoices](/docs/pricing/download-invoices).

## Download your invoices (pricing/download-invoices)

Find and download PDF invoices for payments made from your Bolna account.

You can download invoices for your Bolna account from the dashboard. This works for individual accounts as well as accounts in an organization.

**Open your invoices**:
    Sign in to the [Bolna dashboard](https://platform.bolna.ai). In the left sidebar, expand **Settings → Organisation** and select **Invoices**. You can also go directly to [Invoices](https://platform.bolna.ai/account?tab=invoices).

**Find the payment**:
    Locate the invoice by its **Invoice ID**, date, item type, amount, or status. Use the filters above the table if you have many invoices.

**Download the PDF**:
    Click the download icon in the **Download invoice** column of that invoice's row. Your browser will open or download the PDF, depending on its settings.

  An unpaid postpaid invoice may show **Pay now** in the **Action** column. The PDF download is available for eligible payments after they have been processed. If you cannot find an invoice, clear the filters and check again; for further help, contact [support@bolna.ai](mailto:support@bolna.ai).

## Buying a Pilot plan (pricing/pilot-plan)

Buy a Bolna Pilot plan directly from the dashboard — discounted per-minute rates, higher concurrency, and one month of guided support.

## What is the pilot plan?

The **Pilot plan** is a one-time purchase that bundles discounted minutes with one month of guided support, so you can build and launch your first agents with help from a Bolna point of contact (POC).

It is available to new accounts that are still on the standard pay-as-you-go rate. If your workspace already has a volume or enterprise plan, the Pilot tab isn't shown — [contact us](mailto:enterprise@bolna.ai) instead.

## Pilot tiers

| Minutes | Price | Effective rate | Concurrency |
| - | - | - | - |
| 6,500 mins | \$300 | \~\$0.046/min | 25 |
| 12,000 mins | \$500 | \~\$0.042/min | 25 |

Both tiers include:

* Priority support via a dedicated WhatsApp group
* 1:1 calls with your POC
* Enterprise features enabled
* Maximum concurrency of 25
* The same [preferred models](/docs/pricing/preferred-models) bundled into the flat rate

  Minutes bought under a Pilot land in your normal wallet balance and don't expire. Once they are consumed, the account reverts to standard platform pricing, starting at \$0.06/min.

## How to buy a pilot

**Open the Add Funds panel**:
    Go to [platform.bolna.ai/dashboard](https://platform.bolna.ai/dashboard) and click the **+** button next to your wallet balance in the top-right corner of the Agent Studio header.

**Switch to the Pilot tab**:
    Select the **Pilot** tab next to **Add Funds**. If you don't see it, your account isn't eligible for a Pilot.

**Pick a tier**:
    Choose either the 6,500-minute or 12,000-minute option. The per-minute rate and savings update with your selection.

**Complete checkout**:
    Click **Buy a pilot** to go to Stripe checkout. Your Pilot starts once the payment clears, and the minutes are credited to your wallet.

  You can jump straight to Pilot checkout with the direct link [platform.bolna.ai/checkout/pilot](https://platform.bolna.ai/checkout/pilot). Closing the dialog returns you to the dashboard.

## Terms and conditions

Purchasing a Pilot means accepting the Bolna Pilot Program terms, which cover the support period, pricing validity, refunds, and call-recording obligations.

**Pilot program terms and conditions**:
  Read the full published terms for the Bolna Pilot Program.

Key points:

* The dedicated support period runs for **one month** from the date your payment clears.
* Your Pilot per-minute rate applies until the purchased minutes are fully consumed, even after the support month ends.
* The Pilot fee is **non-refundable** once paid, except where the law requires otherwise.
* Call recordings are mandatory during the Pilot.

## Preferred models included in the flat rate (pricing/preferred-models)

See exactly which ASR, LLM, and TTS models are bundled into Bolna's flat per-minute rate, versus which ones trigger variable provider costs.

## What are preferred models?

Every Bolna workspace ships with a **flat per-minute rate** — **\$0.06/min (₹5.52/min)** at standard wallet tiers — that already includes a curated set of transcriber (ASR), LLM, and voice (TTS) models. As long as your agent uses only these models, your Voice AI cost is the flat rate — you don't see separate line items for STT, LLM, or TTS usage.

If you pick a model outside this list (a premium model, a [BYOK provider](/docs/pricing/call-pricing#how-can-i-reduce-my-voice-ai-costs), or a model not yet marked as preferred), that component is billed at variable, usage-based rates instead of the flat rate. This is the single biggest reason two agents with the same call volume can see very different bills.

  Preferred models change over time as Bolna negotiates provider rates and adds new options. This page is a snapshot — always check the **Add Funds** panel in your dashboard for the current list on your account.

  Larger wallet top-ups (e.g. \$600+) get a lower effective per-minute rate as a volume discount — the preferred model bundle itself is the same across tiers.

## Where to see your preferred models

**Open Agent Studio and click the wallet '+' button**:
    Go to [platform.bolna.ai/dashboard](https://platform.bolna.ai/dashboard) and click the **+** button next to your wallet balance and remaining minutes in the top-right corner of the Agent Studio header.

**Open the Add Funds panel**:
    This opens the **Add Funds** modal. Scroll to the **"Your Selection"** section below the top-up amount options.

**Click 'Show models'**:
    Under **"Your preferred models (included in the 6¢/min rate)"**, click **Show models** to expand the panel. It lists every ASR, LLM, and TTS model bundled into your current rate, grouped by category.

**Cross-check against your agent config**:
    Compare this list with the transcriber, LLM, and voice selected on your agent's [Languages Tab](/docs/agent-setup/audio-tab) and [Intelligence Tab](/docs/agent-setup/llm-tab). If your agent uses a model not on this list, expect variable pricing for that component.

## Why does this matter for my bill?

**On the flat rate**:
    Agent uses only preferred ASR + LLM + TTS models → you pay the flat per-minute rate plus telephony and platform fee. No separate Voice AI usage line items.

**Off the flat rate**:
    Agent uses any non-preferred model → that component (STT, LLM, or TTS) is billed at variable, usage-based rates on top of telephony and platform fee. See the [call pricing breakdown](/docs/pricing/call-pricing#what-does-call-pricing-depend-on).

  You can mix and match: for example, run a preferred LLM with a non-preferred TTS voice. Only the non-preferred component is billed separately — everything else stays on the flat rate.
